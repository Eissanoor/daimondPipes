About Diamond PVC

Our history, mission, vision and commitment to dependable plastic piping solutions.

History & Introduction

Progress through manufacturing experience and continuous improvement.

Diamond PVC Pipe Industry (Pvt) Ltd. began in 2012 as M/s Muhammad Sardar PVC Pipe Industry, initially manufacturing PVC and uPVC pipes. The company later expanded its capabilities and product range to include HDPE, MDPE, PPRC pipes and fittings under the SKYFIT brand.

Our manufacturing plant is located at Plot No. 42, 43 & 47, Main Road Industrial Estate, Gadoon Amazai, District Swabi, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Pakistan. The organization serves dealers, contractors, public-sector departments, agricultural customers, builders and private-sector projects with a focus on consistent quality and dependable supply.

2012 - Foundation

Operations began as M/s Muhammad Sardar PVC Pipe Industry with a focus on PVC and uPVC pipes.

Expansion of Product Range

The organization expanded into HDPE, MDPE, PPRC pipes, fittings and related piping systems.

Diamond PVC & SKYFIT

The company developed its corporate identity as Diamond PVC Pipe Industry (Pvt) Ltd. with products marketed under SKYFIT.
