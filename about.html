Company Profile

Professional pipe manufacturing for domestic, commercial and industrial sectors

Diamond PVC Pipe Industry Pvt Ltd is a professional pipe manufacturing company engaged in the production of PVC, HDPE, PPRC pipes and fittings. The company aims to provide reliable piping solutions for domestic, commercial, agricultural and industrial sectors.

Our manufacturing plant is equipped with modern machinery and a dedicated technical team. We focus on producing pipes that provide excellent performance, long life, smooth water flow and resistance against pressure, corrosion and environmental conditions.

We believe in quality, honesty, innovation and customer satisfaction.

Company Introduction

Trusted and progressive pipe manufacturing since 2012

Diamond PVC Pipe Industry Pvt Ltd is a trusted and progressive pipe manufacturing company with a strong foundation in quality, reliability and customer satisfaction.

The company was established in 2012 under the name M/s Muhammad Sardar PVC Pipe Industry, initially focusing on the production of PVC and UPVC pipes. With dedication, continuous improvement and growing customer confidence, the company expanded its manufacturing capabilities and later transformed into Diamond PVC Pipe Industry Pvt Ltd.

Today, Diamond PVC Pipe Industry Pvt Ltd is engaged in the manufacturing of PVC, UPVC, PPRC and HDPE pipes and fittings, serving the needs of agriculture, construction, water supply, plumbing, sewerage, drainage and industrial sectors. The company also manufactures quality PPRC pipes and fittings under the brand name SKYFIT, designed to provide dependable solutions for hot and cold water supply systems.

Our commitment is to deliver products that meet modern requirements for strength, durability and long-lasting performance. Backed by modern machinery, skilled technical staff and strict quality control procedures, Diamond PVC Pipe Industry Pvt Ltd continues to provide reliable piping solutions to customers, dealers, contractors and development projects across the region.

We believe that our growth is the result of customer trust, product quality and our continuous effort to improve. As a responsible manufacturing organization, we remain committed to innovation, professional service and excellence in every product we deliver.
