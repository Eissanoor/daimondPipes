We manufacture a complete range of pipe and fitting products for different sectors.

PVC Pipes

PVC pipes are widely used for water supply, drainage, irrigation, sewerage and construction purposes. These pipes are lightweight, durable, easy to install and resistant to corrosion.

Features

  • Strong and durable
  • Smooth internal surface
  • Corrosion resistant
  • Lightweight and easy to handle
  • Long service life
  • Suitable for water and drainage systems

Applications

  • Water supply
  • Agriculture
  • Sewerage
  • Drainage
  • Construction
  • Domestic plumbing

HDPE Pipes

HDPE pipes are known for their flexibility, strength and high-pressure resistance. They are suitable for underground water supply, irrigation, gas lines, industrial use and long-distance water transportation.

Features

  • High pressure resistance
  • Flexible and strong
  • Leak-proof joints
  • Chemical resistant
  • Suitable for underground installation
  • Long-lasting performance

Applications

  • Water supply networks
  • Agriculture irrigation
  • Industrial pipelines
  • Sewerage systems
  • Bore wells
  • Underground piping systems

PPRC Pipes

PPRC pipes are commonly used for hot and cold water supply systems. These pipes are safe, hygienic and suitable for residential and commercial plumbing.

Features

  • Suitable for hot and cold water
  • Rust-free and corrosion-free
  • Hygienic for drinking water
  • Strong jointing system
  • Long life
  • Smooth water flow

Applications

  • Residential plumbing
  • Commercial buildings
  • Hot water supply
  • Cold water supply
  • Hospitals
  • Hotels, schools and offices
