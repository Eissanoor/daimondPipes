Manufacturing Process

Our pipe manufacturing process is carried out using modern machinery and skilled operators.

Production Steps

From raw material to finished product

Step 1: Raw Material Preparation

Quality raw material is selected and prepared for production.

Step 2: Extrusion Process

The material is processed through extrusion machinery to form pipes.

Step 3: Cooling and Sizing

The pipe is cooled and sized according to required dimensions.

Step 4: Cutting

Pipes are cut into required lengths.

Step 5: Printing and Marking

Product details such as size, pressure class and company name may be printed.

Step 6: Testing

Pipes are tested for quality, strength and dimensions.

Step 7: Packing and Dispatch

Approved products are packed and supplied to customers.
