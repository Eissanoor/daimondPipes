Testing Workflow

From material receipt to final dispatch

01

Incoming Material

Raw material and relevant batch information are reviewed before use.

02

Process Monitoring

Production parameters, appearance and dimensional consistency are monitored during extrusion and moulding.

03

Laboratory Checks

Selected samples may undergo hydrostatic, tensile, dimensional and related checks according to product requirements.

04

Final Inspection

Marking, finishing, packing and batch identification are reviewed before dispatch.
