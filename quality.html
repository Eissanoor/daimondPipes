Our Quality Control Process

From raw material to final approval

01

Raw Material Inspection

We inspect raw material before production to ensure product quality.

02

Production Monitoring

Our technical staff monitors the manufacturing process during production.

03

Dimensional Testing

Pipe diameter, wall thickness and length are checked carefully.

04

Pressure Testing

Pipes are tested to ensure they can perform under required pressure.

05

Visual Inspection

Final products are checked for finishing, surface quality and defects.

06

Final Approval

Only approved products are packed and dispatched to customers.
