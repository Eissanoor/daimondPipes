Quality Assurance

Structured testing for dependable production

Our quality process covers incoming material, production monitoring, dimensional checks, pressure testing and final inspection before dispatch.

01

Raw Material Verification

Approved material formulations and batch identification are checked before production.

02

Dimensional Inspection

Pipe diameter, wall thickness, length and surface finishing are checked during production.

03

Hydrostatic Pressure Testing

Selected samples are checked to assess pressure performance and product integrity.

04

Final Product Review

Visual finishing, marking, packing and batch details are reviewed before dispatch.
